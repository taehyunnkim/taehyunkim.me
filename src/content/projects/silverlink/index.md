## Overview

SilverLink is a mobile app concept that helps elderly users discover and join local social events, reducing isolation by making it easy to find people nearby with shared interests.

## Design Focus

- **Accessibility first** — large type scale, high-contrast palette, and generous touch targets sized for less precise input.
- **Low-friction onboarding** — interest and availability captured in a few steps, with no dead-end forms.
- **Event discovery** — browsing by proximity, time of day, and activity type instead of an endless feed.
- **Trust signals** — host profiles, group size, and venue details surfaced before the user commits.
